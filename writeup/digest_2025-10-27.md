## The Elephant in the Room: Security Theater vs. Real Risk

Vitalik's warning about blockchain security only protecting onchain assets is getting buried in the noise, but it's the actual red pill nobody wants to talk about. Your crypto is safe on-chain until it isn't—and "isn't" happens the moment it goes through a validator network that depends on human trust. This is the validator collusion risk that never gets solved by cryptography alone. It's the problem that keeps scaling solutions fragile no matter how fancy the math is. As Ethereum and other networks scale through validators, this becomes the actual attack surface. Expect more discussions around this as the ecosystem matures.
