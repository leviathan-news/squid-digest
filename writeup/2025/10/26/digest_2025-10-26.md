Here's what everyone's missing: we've spent years talking about Ethereum's security through decentralization, but then we built an entire scaling solution layer on top of centralized cloud infrastructure. Base getting wrecked while Solana chugged along? That's not a Solana flex—it's a warning that we've recreated the same single point of failure problem we left traditional finance to escape. The irony is so thick you could stake it.

This matters because it exposes a tier system in crypto that contradicts the entire value prop. You've got truly decentralized chains, then you've got "decentralized" chains that are actually dependent on one cloud provider's uptime. If you're holding significant positions in L2s, you need to understand that your censorship resistance ends the moment AWS sneezes.
