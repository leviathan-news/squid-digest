### 3. The UK Doubles Down on Encryption Backdoors for Apple Users

Despite earlier backlash, the UK government is again demanding Apple create a backdoor in its iCloud backup encryption for British users. Citing “security” under Technical Capability Notices, the move would force Apple to either weaken end-to-end encryption or remove protections for UK customers entirely—a chilling signpost for privacy rights. The EFF warns these maneuvers endanger user safety and invite global copycats.

**Significance:** These policy battles aren’t just about Apple or the UK—they’re a beacon for the crypto-privacy movement worldwide, affirming why decentralized, censorship-resistant infrastructure is more needed (and more at risk) than ever.
